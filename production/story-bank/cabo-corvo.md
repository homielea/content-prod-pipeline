# Story bank — The Real Housewives of Cabo Corvo

Feedstock for the production pipeline. Entries are written by the
**story-catcher** skill in the brand's locked entry format and reviewed by a
human against the Fictionalization Firewall before production.

Statuses: `new` (caught, unreviewed) → `approved` (human-cleared for
production) → `produced` (episode shipped).

Firewall reminder: no real names, no real places, composites only. If an entry
reads as a specific real person, it goes back to story-catcher for harder
blurring — it does not get approved.
